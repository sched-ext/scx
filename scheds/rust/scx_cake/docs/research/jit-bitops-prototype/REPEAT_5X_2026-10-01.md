# Native scalar comparison: five repeats — 2026-10-01

**40/40 slots passed; 1,247,480 simulated frames retained.** Five mirrored
eight-slot blocks supplied ten 15-second observations per mode. No verifier
failure, ignored flags, stall, abnormal exit or failed native restoration
occurred. The kernel journal independently records 40 enables and 40 normal
disables, with no BUG, WARNING, Oops or watchdog event.

The larger screen consistently finds higher BPF CPU time with division-free.
Bitops-only shows small callback savings and a small aggregate reduction,
but no clear overall CPU-time or simulated-frame improvement. Neither flag
was enabled by default. This remains a diagnostic cost experiment.

- Orders: ABCDDCBA, DCBAABCD, ABCDDCBA, DCBAABCD, ABCDDCBA.

- No scheduler source changes or builds occurred during capture.

## Noise

from 19.80% in the earlier screen. Total slot CPU busy averaged 37.22%,
range 36.61–38.31%. Estimated non-appsim busy averaged 5.82%, range

host busy time; it includes desktop processes plus kernel/harness overhead,

## Paired block variation

Exploratory 95% percentile intervals resample the five whole blocks,
20,000 times with seed 20261001. Individual frames are correlated:
1.25 million frames are not 1.25 million independent experiments.
Five blocks give limited precision; these intervals are not adjusted
for multiple comparisons and do not authorize default promotion.

| Mode | BPF CPU change, 95% interval | Blocks with lower BPF CPU | p99 delta ns, 95% interval |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Native bitops | -1.13% to +0.16% | 3/5 | -1,730 to +530 |
| Division-free | +3.88% to +7.80% | 0/5 | -2,090 to +3,670 |
| Both | +4.33% to +7.71% | 0/5 | -2,250 to +530 |

Division-free and both modes increased BPF CPU time in every block.
All p99 and p99.9 change intervals include zero. Average FPS remains
approximately 2,079 in all modes, matching the configured cadence;
this is not evidence about uncapped throughput or actual game FPS.
